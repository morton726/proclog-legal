ProcLog Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 20, 2026

ProcLog ("ProcLog," "the App," "we," "us," or "our") is a personal procedure-logging application published by C.A.R.E., designed for individual medical providers to document and organize their own professional experience records. This Privacy Policy explains what information ProcLog handles, how it is used, whether and how it is shared, and the choices available to you.

ProcLog is intended for individual professional use. It is not designed, offered, or maintained as a hospital, clinic, health system, payer, or enterprise credentialing platform. ProcLog is not an electronic health record, designated record set, or employer-managed medical staff system, and is not provided on behalf of a covered entity or healthcare organization. You are responsible for complying with your employer's, facility's, licensing body's, and professional privacy obligations — including your institution's device, data-handling, and patient-information policies — when choosing what information to enter into the App.

1. Information ProcLog Handles

ProcLog has no account system, no login, and no developer-operated server. There is nothing to sign up for and nothing transmitted to us. The information ProcLog handles falls into two categories:

  • Local profile information. A name and professional credential you enter during setup (e.g. "MD," "PA," "RN"), used only to label your own exports and displayed only to you.
  • User-entered log data. Procedure names, dates, codes, locations, supervision levels, outcomes, notes, and any identifiers or patient-related details you choose to enter.

ProcLog does not collect account credentials, email addresses, passwords, analytics, advertising identifiers, or device diagnostics. The App contains no analytics SDK, no crash-reporting SDK, and no advertising library of any kind.

All of the information above is stored locally on your device using your operating system's on-device storage. If you enable cloud backup, ProcLog additionally stores a backup of this same information in your own personal cloud account — your iCloud account on iOS, or your Google Drive account on Android — so your data can be restored if you get a new device. On Android this is optional and requires you to connect a Google account explicitly; the backup is written to a private application folder in your Drive, not to your visible Drive files. In either case the backup is created directly by your device communicating with Apple's or Google's service — it never passes through, or becomes visible to, C.A.R.E. or ProcLog's developer.

Subscription and purchase information. If you subscribe to ProcLog Pro, the purchase transaction is handled entirely by your platform's app store — Apple's App Store on iOS, or Google Play on Android — through that store's in-app purchase system. ProcLog receives only a purchase/entitlement status (subscribed or not) from the platform's in-app purchase framework (Apple StoreKit or Google Play Billing), used solely to unlock unlimited logging — it never receives your name, email, or payment/card details, because neither store shares those with the app. Apple's or Google's own handling of your payment information is governed by their respective privacy policies, not this one.

2. How This Information Is Used

Because ProcLog has no server and no account system, information is used exclusively to:

  • display, organize, and search your own procedure logs, on your own device;
  • generate PDF/CSV exports at your explicit request;
  • back up and restore your data via your own cloud account (iCloud or Google Drive), if enabled;
  • schedule optional local reminder notifications on your device;
  • unlock unlimited procedure logging once the App Store or Google Play confirms an active ProcLog Pro subscription.

ProcLog does not use your information for advertising, does not build profiles about you, and does not use your information to train any model.

3. How Information Is Shared

ProcLog does not sell information, and there is no third party for us to share it with — we do not operate servers that receive your data in the first place. Specifically:

  • No service providers receive your log data. ProcLog does not use analytics vendors, advertising networks, or third-party cloud hosting for your log data. The only infrastructure involved is your own platform's cloud backup (Apple iCloud or Google Drive) and your own platform's in-app purchase system (Apple StoreKit or Google Play Billing), both acting under your own Apple ID or Google Account and governed by Apple's or Google's own privacy terms — not a vendor relationship of ours.
  • User-directed sharing only. When you export or share a report, that file is generated on your device and shared only through your operating system's share sheet, to a destination you personally choose (e.g. email, a messaging app, or a file you save). We have no visibility into, or control over, what you do with an exported file.
  • Legal and safety reasons. Because ProcLog holds no copy of your data, we would generally have nothing to produce in response to a legal request directed at us — your data lives on your device and in your own cloud account, both under your control.

4. Sensitive and Patient-Related Information

ProcLog is designed as a personal professional tool for individual clinicians. Avoid entering unnecessary patient identifiers, and use the minimum information needed for your own documentation purposes — for example, a partial medical record number (MRN) rather than a full one, if your institution's policy allows. If you choose to enter patient-related information, you are solely responsible for ensuring you are permitted to do so under your workplace, professional, and legal obligations.

ProcLog is not represented as a covered entity, healthcare provider, electronic health record, or hospital credentialing platform, and is not provided on behalf of a hospital, clinic, health system, health plan, or other covered entity unless expressly stated otherwise in a separate written agreement.

5. Data Retention

Your log data remains on your device until you delete it, delete the app, or use the in-app "Delete account & data" feature. If cloud backup is enabled, a copy also persists in your personal cloud account until you delete it — either via "Delete account & data" in the app, or directly through your platform's own storage management (iCloud storage settings under your Apple ID on iOS, or your Google Account's app-data management on Android). C.A.R.E. retains none of your log data anywhere, at any time, because it never reaches us.

6. Data Security

ProcLog applies security measures appropriate for sensitive clinical information, including:

  • on-device-only storage, protected by your operating system's platform-level encryption;
  • an optional biometric App Lock (Face ID/Touch ID on iOS, fingerprint or face unlock on Android), handled entirely by your device's secure hardware — Apple's Secure Enclave or the Android Keystore — so biometric data never leaves your device and is never accessible to ProcLog;
  • an on-screen privacy mode to obscure sensitive fields from view;
  • cloud backup secured under Apple's or Google's own encryption and account-security infrastructure.

No method of storage is completely immune to risk, and we cannot guarantee absolute security of a device that is lost, stolen, or compromised — enabling a device passcode and App Lock is strongly recommended.

7. Your Choices and Controls

Because your data lives on your device and in your own cloud account, you have direct, immediate control over it at all times — no request to us is required. Within the app you can view, edit, export, and permanently delete your data, including a full "Delete account & data" action that removes both the on-device copy and the cloud backup. For general questions, you may still contact us using the information in Section 11.

8. Children's Privacy

ProcLog is intended for licensed medical professionals, trainees, and adult healthcare professionals, and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

9. International Use

ProcLog is currently intended for users in the United States. If the App later expands internationally, supplemental disclosures may apply based on local law.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If changes are material, we will update the Effective Date above and, where appropriate, provide notice through the App.
