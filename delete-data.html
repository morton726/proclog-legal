Delete Your ProcLog Data

How to delete data in ProcLog, published by C.A.R.E.

You can delete everything yourself, from inside the app, at any time. ProcLog has no account system and no developer-operated server — your data is never sent to us, so there is no request to submit and nothing for us to delete on your behalf. Deletion is immediate and under your control.

How to delete all of your ProcLog data

  1. Open the ProcLog app.
  2. Tap your profile icon in the top-right corner of the Quick Log screen.
  3. Scroll to the bottom of the Profile screen.
  4. Tap Delete Account (shown in red).
  5. Confirm when prompted. This cannot be undone.

This single action permanently removes your on-device data and the backup held in your own cloud account, if you enabled cloud backup.

What is deleted

DataWhat happens
Your profile (name, credential, NPI, specialty, institution)Deleted from the device immediately.
All logged procedure entries, including dates, codes, locations, supervision levels, outcomes, notes, and any MRNs you enteredDeleted from the device immediately.
Pinned procedures, custom procedures, and your in-app activity logDeleted from the device immediately.
Cloud backup (iCloud on iOS, Google Drive on Android), if enabledDeleted from your own cloud account as part of the same action.

What is kept, and for how long

C.A.R.E. retains none of your ProcLog data, at any time — because it never reaches us. There is no server-side copy, no analytics record, and no backup held by the developer, so there is no additional retention period after you delete.

Two things sit outside the app and are not affected by deleting your data:

  • Subscription records. If you subscribed to ProcLog Pro, the purchase record is held by Apple or Google, not by us. Manage or cancel a subscription in the App Store or Google Play; deleting your app data does not cancel a subscription.
  • Files you exported yourself. Any PDF or CSV report you exported and saved or shared lives wherever you chose to put it, and must be deleted there by you.

Deleting the app instead

Uninstalling ProcLog removes the on-device data. If you enabled cloud backup, a copy may remain in your own cloud account until you delete it — either by using Delete Account in the app before uninstalling, or through your platform's own storage management (iCloud storage settings under your Apple ID on iOS, or your Google Account's app-data management on Android).
